# Wiring Instructions for the Plant Panel

The plant panel is meant to be close enough to the CFC that it can be directly wired. The direct connections are:
1. The Line Voltage (heater) connections and Grounding
2. The Fan Connector
3. The Thermocouples
4. The High Amperage, Low-voltage stack power

Although simple, some adive may be helpful here.

I choose to use 18" wires for all the wires that I controlled, except the fan wires, which are 24" long (because the must reach the fan, which is further away from the top of the CFC.

## The Line Voltage (heater) connections and Grounding

I found it convenient to use Spade-type connectors here.

Note that the heater has no ground; the ground connection in a 3-prong connector is connected to the CFC chassis with a wire of the same ampacity as the line voltage.
